The next season

A working game.
Room to grow.

The career, transfers, training, youth, competitions and 2D/3D matches already work. The next steps build on that foundation.

What you can play today

A management career across 22 leagues and 440 clubs, with domestic and continental football, national teams, financial decisions, scouting, transfers and player development. Matches can be watched in 2D/3D or played with direct control.

Improve what players already use

Navigation, a command palette, onboarding, six themes, scaling and rebindable keys already exist. The first priority is to find and remove friction in five real journeys: start a career, choose a lineup, change tactics, negotiate a signing and play a match. Fix unclear decisions, clipped layouts and keyboard access, then retest with newcomers.

A reliable download and trustworthy football

Finish platform release validation and test packaged games without development tools. Investigate current match calibration failures with equal and unequal teams and complete seasons. Build a player-facing practice match and national-team live matchday on the existing engine; extend set-piece interaction beyond the existing pass-button restart release; improve country-specific calendars without replacing the competitions that already run.

Create your own football world, in useful stages

Custom JSON data is available today. The next step is a safe in-game editor, not a promise that arbitrary worlds already work.

  1. Edit supported players, clubs and leagues for a new career, with previews, validation and recoverable drafts.
  2. Export and import versioned packs with authorship and conflict reporting.
  3. Add explicit countries and associations beyond the current league-pyramid model, including nationality, national teams and calendars.
  4. Expose existing competition formats, then extend reusable rules for club and national leagues, cups, qualifiers and finals. Preview schedules and validate a complete tournament and the next season.

Build on the existing preseason planner

Choosing friendly opponents, home/away venues, tours, camps and assistant suggestions already works. Extend that with club invitations and replies, confirmed dates and terms, matches for a stated cause with in-game proceeds, and small invited summer tournaments. Availability, recovery, cancellations and financial settlement must stay consistent. These are career features; online multiplayer and real-world fundraising are separate ideas.

A community beyond the code

Contributor guides, credits and player cameos already exist. Build on them with one maintained community space, a named organiser, clear participation rules and focused playtests. Welcome guides, translations, data and accessibility feedback; help a newcomer finish a first contribution and show what changed because of player feedback.

These are proposed outcomes, not available features or promised release dates. The detailed roadmap separates today's capabilities, incremental steps and the larger design work they depend on.
