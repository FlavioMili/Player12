Detailed Description

The leg sweep's scan view (game xG plan, layer 3), in the xG map tool: the latest Shot block runs as they are measured. On the left, the whole shot in its own frame (the shooter at the bottom, the goal at the top): the aim line, the ball's actual path coloured by what came of it, the defender as it was struck with his velocity as an arrow coloured by how he moves, and a ghost where he'd have been as it passed him; the runs before it fade behind. On the right, close in round the ghost: the offset bands the run counts in, shaded by the chances measured so far, and the gap; below, the table for the kind and rating being measured, filling in.
