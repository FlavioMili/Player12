How hard a contact is (contact plan §2): meeting the ball at moment to send it along aim (metric, unit, seen from above) at intendedSpeed over the ground and intendedLift upward (m/s). surprise: how little he expected it (0-1, the brain's read; 0 for his own ball or one he chose to strike).
