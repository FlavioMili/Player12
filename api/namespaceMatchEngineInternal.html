A body moving from from to to (metres) kept where it can physically be round the pitch (MatchGround): inside the stands' front walls, off the boards, the dugout fronts and the tunnel walls. The gaps in the boards let him through to the technical areas, the dugouts (at their open ends) and down the tunnel. An axis left alone is returned exactly.
