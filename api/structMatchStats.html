◆ ballBodyHitsByPart

std::array<int, static_cast<std::size_t>(ShotBodyPart::COUNT)> MatchStats::ballBodyHitsByPart {}

Check (shot block plan §3f): shots whose path, against the player's own move over the sub-step, went well inside a Binturong torso without meeting him (one per shot and player; should be none), and of those, the ones already inside at a sub-step's start. ◆ collisionKnocksBySituation

std::array<int, 8> MatchStats::collisionKnocksBySituation {}

Stumbles and falls by the situation they came from (the knocked player's view; the first that fits): 0 a re-hit (the same pair within 1 s), 1 both at the ball (within 2 m of the contact), 2 his run aimed at the other (his target within 1.5 m of him), 3 an overtake of the other under way, 4 a race to the same spot, the same way, not being overtaken, 5 either standing or slow (under 1 m/s), 6 paths crossing or head-on (over 45 degrees apart), 7 anything else; and by the knocked player's intent.
