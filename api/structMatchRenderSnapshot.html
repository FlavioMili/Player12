◆ zones

std::vector<MatchRenderZone> MatchRenderSnapshot::zones

A training session's view of its ground (training ground plan section 23): the pitch above is the ground, everything else is already placed on it, and these are its zones (walls and goals drawn at each) and the other zones' balls. Empty otherwise.
