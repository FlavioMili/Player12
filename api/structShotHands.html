Detailed Description

A keeper's hands as a shot met them (goalkeeper plan step 2): held ready in front of him, then, once he has reacted, both side by side to where its line passes him (the nearer one alone when the other can't reach), with a miss. Positions are metres from his centre (x, y) and up from the ground (z); targets are on the pitch (metres). Index 0 is his left hand.
