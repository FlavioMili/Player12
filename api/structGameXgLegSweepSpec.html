Detailed Description

Game xG layer 3's leg chance (game xG plan, step 3): the Shot block drill with one body near the shot's line, over his distance from the shooter, how far the line passes from him, his blocking rating (Defending and Tackling together), how he moves and whose he is. The chance a shot through his leg-space meets a leg: leg meetings over the runs whose ball didn't meet his torso or head. The offset is how far his path (where he would have been had he not reacted) ran from the ball's actual path, not from the line aimed along: aims run over the offsets and each run counts at the offset nearest its measured one. The shooter's own error is measured apart (the scatter phase: Shooting levels, the defender well off the line) for the estimate to spread its aim lines by. The shooter stands GAP metres further out than him, aiming low, so the ball is low as it reaches him. Team-mates (no leg put out, so no rating) are measured at the middle rating and stored at every one.
