Detailed Description

Where a shot meets his body (shot block plan §3c-3): how far along the ball's stretch (0-1), the part, the surface's outward normal there (unit, metres), how fast that surface is moving (m/s: his own velocity, and a leg put out still going out), and whether it is the foot of a leg he put out (it may aim a little).
