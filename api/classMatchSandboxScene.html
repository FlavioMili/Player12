Detailed Description

Setup screen of the match sandbox (match_sandbox): two clubs of a throwaway world, both sides' selection and tactics, then a live match.

The home side is the managed one: the live match offers its touchline (substitutions, tactics, shouts) and Play mode. The away side is played by the AI with the tactics set here. Nothing is recorded: full time returns here with the score, ready for a rematch.
