The smoothed chance at (x, y) metres from data (game xG plan section 8): goals over shots, pooled under a Gaussian kernel over the pitch (width 0.75 of the distance to the 4th-nearest spot, the local spacing, at least 0.5 m) times one over the mouth angle (width 10 degrees), so spots that see the goal very differently (either side of a post on the goal line) don't pool.
