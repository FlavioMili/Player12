Detailed Description

Shots (step 3c; shooting technique plan step 3, 2026-10-24: first guesses). Skill: Shooting, cutting the errors as a pass's by Passing (0.9). Power costs precision: the power asked (pace against his shot power, nothing up to 70% of it, steeply beyond) adds to the angle job and the weight job, in place of the pace change against a fixed 40 m/s. The jitter grows with the angle job's difficulty from a small floor (15%), so an easy strike is tight and the spread comes from power, pressure and the rest; 0.375 of it vertical. On top, failures grow with the conditions: usually skied, a failed weight scuffed.
