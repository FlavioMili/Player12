Detailed Description

Persistent career identity, attributes, traits and day-to-day state.

On-pitch positions, intentions and live fatigue belong to MatchPlayer in match_engine.h. The engine borrows this Player as read-only input and the career later applies its match consequences. PlayerAgent models contract negotiations, not on-pitch AI. See docs/development/player-behavior.md. Concurrent const queries may refresh the price cache. Attribute mutations require exclusive career ownership, as with other persistent model state.

Increases the player's age by 1 and applies yearly ageing.
