Detailed Description

The xG map (training ground plan section 24, T8): a pitch, the match one or a training pitch of any size and goal kind, shaded by the engine's own xG for a shooter attacking the right-hand goal, with contours; or by the core of the chosen brain's shot score (the home side's brain from the sandbox set-up: Capybara's own, else Binturong's). Hovering shows every part of both at that spot. Settings: Shooting, foot or header, a defender at a set distance, the space ahead.
