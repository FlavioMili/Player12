◆ BodyId

enum class BodyId : std::uint8_t
strong

A side's "body": how one player's body carries out what his brain chose and what it costs him (movement, fitness, kicking - where he meets the ball, how his strike comes off, a free kick against the wall - dribbling, first touch, goalkeeping, non-contact injuries). Contests between players, the ball's flight, the rules and attribute mapping are shared by every body.

AARDVARK is the inherited body, kept frozen for comparison; the BINTURONG and later bodies are the ones being developed. Each side picks one before kickoff (MatchEngine::setBody), independently of its brain (match_brain.h).
