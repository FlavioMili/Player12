Detailed Description

Small-sided game (training ground plan section 22, T6): the brain redesign's ladder (1 player, 1 v 1, 2 v 2, 3 v 3, N v N) on a walled training pitch sized by area per player, goals at the ends, the coach serving to the home side to start and after every goal. Reports what each rung is there to show; measures, not targets.
