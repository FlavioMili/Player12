◆ ManagerId

enum class ManagerId : std::uint8_t
strong

A side's "AI manager": the touchline decisions the engine makes for a side it manages (MatchEngine::setAutoSubstitutions) - which players to take off and when (injury, fatigue, cards, the score, medical staff limits), who comes on, and the shouts. Carrying out a change (the substitution itself, the shape rebalanced, a forced keeper) is shared by every manager.

AARDVARK is the inherited manager, kept frozen for comparison; the BINTURONG and later managers are the ones being developed. Each side picks one before kickoff (MatchEngine::setManager), independently of its brain and body.
