Detailed Description

Academies of every club: yearly intake cycle, U18 and U21 squads and their leagues, youth contracts and board-funded academy projects.

Calendar (every club, same rules): the head of youth development previews the intake on 1 February, candidates arrive on 15 March and the manager has 30 days to offer contracts; computer-managed clubs sign their best prospects on intake day. U18 teams play a cheap aggregate league every week of the season; minutes and junior coaching speed up development. U21 (reserve) squads hold players aged 21 or younger plus a few over-age players; they are listed apart from the senior squad like the U18s and play one cheap league per country midweek.
