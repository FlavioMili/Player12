Detailed Description

The game xG sweep (game xG plan, section 8): the Still-ball shot drill run over spots and Shooting ratings, then smoothed into a GameXgTable.

Layer 1, the empty goal: the chance of scoring with no keeper. Layer 2, the keeper: the same against a keeper at his set position, over Goalkeeping ratings too, stored as a factor on layer 1 (the keeper's chance over the empty goal's), so it needs layer 1's map for the goal.

  1. Measure: spots in fixed bands, finest near the goal (each band's step a multiple of the finest), on both sides of the goal.
  2. Symmetry: left and right compared spot by spot; if they agree within noise they are folded together (twice the shots per spot).
  3. Smooth: goals over shots pooled under a Gaussian kernel whose width follows the spacing, onto a regular grid.

The numbers below are our own choices (plan section 8), not research.

◆ bands

std::vector<GameXgBand> GameXgSweepSpec::bands
Initial value:
{
{.lineTo = 18.0F, .sideMax = 20.0F, .step = 1.0F},
{.lineTo = 30.0F, .sideMax = 28.0F, .step = 2.0F},
{.lineTo = 52.0F, .sideMax = 32.0F, .step = 4.0F}}

Finest first: 1 m in and around the box, 2 m to 30 m, 4 m beyond. The last band is the sweep's extent: the match pitch's attacking half (to halfway, 52.5 m out) and as near the touchlines as the drill places the ball, so the map has no flat strips beyond what was measured.
