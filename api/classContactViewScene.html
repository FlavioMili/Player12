Detailed Description

Contact view: how hard a touch or strike is (the Binturong body's assessContact, contact plan §2) across his reach. The disc is his reach circle, seen from above, coloured by where in it he meets the ball; the inner ring by where the ball comes from; the outer ring by where he sends it. The other conditions are set on the panel and shown as bars. Every colour is read from the engine's own function with the default knobs.
