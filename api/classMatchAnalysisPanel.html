Detailed Description

The assistant's half-time / full-time analysis of a live match: where the chances came from, pressing, duels, standout and struggling players, fatigue and two or three suggestions with their evidence.

Self-driving like TeamTalkDialog: call renderForMatch() once per frame. It opens at the break (after the team talk) and at full time, and openNow() shows it on demand. It is a panel in the bottom-right corner, never a modal: the match keeps running and its controls stay usable.
