Detailed Description

First touch (Binturong body, feet): the touch plays the ball on under real physics and the player runs onto it. He owns it from the touch (a claimed ball) until an opponent plays it or play stops (see Notes and plans/match_engine_first_touch_plan.md and match_engine_dribble_touches_plan.md).
