Detailed Description

True view (Notes and plans/match_engine_true_view_plan.md): one player in 3D as the Binturong body models him, with no human figure on top. The body cylinder, the lines between feet, thigh, chest and head, each part's reach with its comfort gap, dead zone and stretch (rings of 12 sections), each technique's own range, jumping, falling and getting up, and the personal space the brain keeps. Every shape is read from the engine's functions and the sandbox's tuning (the contact view's knobs).
