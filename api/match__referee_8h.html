◆ RefereeId

enum class RefereeId : std::uint8_t
strong

The match's "referee": the officials' judgement - how a foul is dealt with (card, advantage, free kick or penalty, denied goal chance), when an advantage ends, whether a pass is given offside and how much time is added. The laws' mechanics are shared by every referee: the offside line and position (the brains read them too), what a card does, the restarts. The per-match strictness is drawn by the handshake (match_handshake.h); the referee applies it.

AARDVARK is the inherited referee, kept frozen for comparison; the BINTURONG and later referees are the ones being developed. One referee per match, chosen before kickoff (MatchEngine::setReferee).
