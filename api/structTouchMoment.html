Detailed Description

A loose ball in a player's reach during one ball sub-step: where it is closest to him on that stretch of its path, how close (m), and his reach (m). Players move only between steps: substepsLeft ball sub-steps of substepSeconds come before his next move, which then comes every stepSeconds.
