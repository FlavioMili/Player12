Detailed Description

Stretch over his reach: a value (0-1) at each angle off his facing (degrees, ascending, 0-180, the same either side) and each distance from his centre as a share of his reach (ascending), blended linearly between neighbours in both, held at the ends. Defaults: MatchTuning::FirstTouch::STRETCH_*.
