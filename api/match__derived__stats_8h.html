std::string_view  statName 
)
inline

One player's own deviation in a stat, in standard deviations: keyed on his id and the stat's name, so it is the same every match and draws nothing from the match's random numbers. A sum of four uniforms (close to a normal, held within +/-3.46) from integer hashing and plain float arithmetic, so it is identical on every platform. Renaming a stat re-deals it.

◆ DEFAULT_DERIVED_RECIPES

constexpr DerivedRecipes DEFAULT_DERIVED_RECIPES
inlineconstexpr

The recipes a handshake uses unless it has its own (the Aardvark's). Future stats: agility and acceleration blend Pace and Physicality, first touch Dribbling and Passing, technique Passing, Dribbling and Shooting, heading Shooting and Defending, jumping reach his height plus a jump from Pace and Physicality, tackling Defending, strength Physicality, balance half Physicality and half Agility's blend, reflexes, handling, throwing and command of area Goalkeeping. The player profile's attributes blend the current stats nearest them, each with a spread of its own (our estimates). Match-engine-only: the endurance stats echo Stamina, except burst capacity (half Stamina, half Acceleration).

◆ PART_TIME_RATING

constexpr float PART_TIME_RATING = 0.45F
inlineconstexpr

Ratings that anchor the physical meanings: a lower-league part-timer and the world's elite (world generation: a league's first-team level is 38 + 0.42 x its reputation; the best clubs reach 90). On the linear scale of the Binturong handshake a rating is the stat / 100; the Aardvark handshake's stretched scale places them differently.
