◆ BallPhysicsId

enum class BallPhysicsId : std::uint8_t
strong

The match's "ball physics": how the ball moves on its own once struck - flight under gravity and air drag, bounces, rolling, the swerve of spin - and the launch solvers that turn "reach there at this speed or height" into a kick, worked out with the same physics so predicted and real flights match. Contacts with players (kicks, touches, saves, rebounds) and what the ball's position means (goals, out of play) are shared by every ball. Brains and bodies predict flights through MatchEngine::stepFlight, so they always use the match's own physics.

AARDVARK is the inherited physics, kept frozen for comparison; the BINTURONG and later ones are the ones being developed. One per match, chosen before kickoff (MatchEngine::setBallPhysics).
