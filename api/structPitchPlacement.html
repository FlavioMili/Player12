Detailed Description

Where a pitch lies on a larger ground (training ground plan section 23): a session's zone, by its centre on the ground, turned 90 degrees or not (its length across the ground), and the pitch's size. Points, vectors and facings go between the pitch's own metres and the ground's. The one copy the session's engines and its views share.
