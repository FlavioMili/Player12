Detailed Description

Under-21 squad: the U21 players with their minutes and contracts (Squad), the country's U21 league with the latest results and the over-age places (League), and the first-team and U18 players who may join the U21s (Candidates). Row actions live in the selected row's detail strip, as on the youth academy screen.
