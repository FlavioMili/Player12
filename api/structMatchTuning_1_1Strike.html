Detailed Description

Strikes carried out by the contact model (Binturong body; Notes and plans/match_engine_contact_plan.md, step 3): each kind's profile, the defaults of the strike knobs (match_strike_knobs.h). The condition levels are on the touch's scale; each condition adds its level to both jobs.
