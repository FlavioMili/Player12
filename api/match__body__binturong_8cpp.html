◆ turnsOutward()

bool turnsOutward ( const ContactSituation &  situation)

Whether sending the ball on along aim from where he meets it turns it outward (likely the outside of his foot) rather than inward (the instep): inward if its path crosses his facing line (his centre straight out the way he faces) before leaving his reach, or leaves his reach between that line and the line from his centre through the ball. A proxy, being tried out in the contact view.
