Detailed Description

The parts of a shot's xG (training ground plan section 24, the xG map): the location model's inputs (metres from the goal line and from the middle, straight distance, the visible angle in radians), its fitted value, the skill and pressure factors, and the result before and after its open-play caps. goal false: the pitch has none (xG 0); inRange false: beyond the fitted range (the floor).
