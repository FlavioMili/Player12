Detailed Description

Layer 3's leg chance (game xG plan, step 3): the chance a body stops a shot whose line passes through its leg-space (below his hip, within his leg's reach of his centre), measured in the Shot block drill. A line through his torso is worked out (a certain block bar a narrow deflection); this is the part the drill has to measure: whether his stride, or a leg put out, gets in the way.

◆ scatterShooting

std::vector<float> GameXgLegTable::scatterShooting

The shooter's error, measured off the same drill (a still ball, no pressure: the least he misses by): for each Shooting (ascending), equal shares of his shots as angles off the line he meant (radians), over the ground (+ to his left; the engine's error there is unsigned, so the points are mirrored) and upward (+ higher). The offsets above are from the ball's actual path, so the estimate spreads each aim line by these.
