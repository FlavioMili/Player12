Detailed Description

Player kinematics: an in-situ acceleration-speed profile a(v) = A0 (1 - v / vmax) for speeding up, a stronger braking capacity and a speed-dependent lateral (centripetal) limit so sharp turns at speed force a player to slow down first.

◆ DIRECTION_TOP_BAND

constexpr std::array<int, 5> MatchTuning::Player::DIRECTION_TOP_BAND {3, 2, 1, 1, 1}
staticconstexpr

Moving at an angle to his body (orientation plan): the eight directions, as the angle between body and run, from 0 (forward) by 45 deg steps to 180 (backward), the same to his left and right. Angles between two of them blend the two. For each direction:

  • the top band he has (MovementMode: 3 sprint, 2 run, 1 jog);
  • his speed as a share of the same forward band;
  • his drive (acceleration) as a share;
  • his energy per metre against forward running. Sources:
  • backward and sideways at 0.7, walk and jog only (the user's call): backward 1.3-1.5x the time over 5-20 m (Sammoud et al. 2023), the 100 m backward record ~70% of the forward record's speed; sideways provisional, no measured figure;
  • energy x1.3: 20-40% more for high-intensity backward and lateral movement (the review cited by Bloomfield et al. 2007);
  • the diagonals harder than their neighbours (the user): 0.6 and x1.4, the forward diagonal up to a run (design choices, to judge on screen);
  • the drive shares (design choices).

◆ PLANT_METRES

constexpr float MatchTuning::Player::PLANT_METRES = 1.5F
staticconstexpr

The plant (sharp reversal): his last PLANT_METRES into the stop are a planted leg's braking, harder than his run's by a share set by Agility (low at 1, high at 99), and he brakes later for it; as he plants, his body twists toward the way out, this far (degrees, by Agility) off his run. Pushing off out of it, his acceleration is up by the same kind of share (by Agility) for PUSH_OFF_SECONDS. All design choices: change-of-direction work has the plant's braking spike and push-off, not these sizes for a body model like this.
