◆ RunResult

enum class GameXgLegSweep::RunResult : std::uint8_t
strong

What came of a run, for the view.

Enumerator
LEG 

Met a leg: a block, counted.

PAST_LOW 

Past him below his hip: counted, no block.

TORSO 

Met his torso or head: not leg-space.

PAST_HIGH 

Past him at his hip or over: not leg-space.

OFF_GRID 

Its path ran wider than the last offset.

SCATTER 

A shooter's-error run (nobody in the way).
