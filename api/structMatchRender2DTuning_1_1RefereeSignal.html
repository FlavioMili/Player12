Detailed Description

The referee's signals: his chip grows to a player's size to show a whistle (red: play stopped; green: restart), a card, play-on for an advantage or an arrow the way the awarded side attacks; and the free-kick spot marked on the grass. Timings are wall-clock seconds (design choices, not researched).
