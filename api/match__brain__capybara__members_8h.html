◆ bodiesPassCapybara()

float MatchEngine::bodiesPassCapybara ( const MatchPlayer &  shooter,
PitchPoint  from 
) const

Game xG layer 3: the chance a shot by shooter from from gets past every body in the way (team-mates too; not the opponents' keeper, layer 2's). Averaged over the aim cone (the lines Capybara's aim can pick, weighted by their chances); along each line, each body where his movement will have him when the ball gets there: through his torso a block (unless it glances off narrowly and still goes in), through his leg-space the leg-chance table's chance; the chances of getting past each multiplied. 1 with no leg-chance table.
