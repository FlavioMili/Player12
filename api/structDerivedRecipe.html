Detailed Description

A derived stat's rating: the weighted blend of current stats and (for a match-engine-only stat) future stats; the weights sum to 1; plus his own deviation (spread x playerStatDeviation); clamped to 0-1.

A stat in metres built on his height (Jumping reach): the blend rates only the part on top of his height, worth heightPartLow metres at STAT_1_RATING to heightPartHigh at STAT_99_RATING (a straight line); his height is added, and the total is rated on the stat's own line. Both 0: not built on his height.

◆ spread

float DerivedRecipe::spread = 0.0F

How far one player's rating strays from the blend: the standard deviation (as a rating, 0.10 = 10 stat points) of his own deviation, keyed on his id so it is the same every match. 0: the blend exactly. The tighter a stat follows the current stats, the smaller.
