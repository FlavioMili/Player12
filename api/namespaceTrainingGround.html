Detailed Description

Training sessions (training ground plan section 23, T7): a squad and a list of exercises; each exercise a set of zones on one 105 x 68 training ground, each zone a drill on its own walled pitch with its own engine and ball, placed on the ground (its centre, turned 0 or 90 degrees), with its players from the squad. Players in no zone wait in the changing room. The zones of an exercise step together at the fixed step.
