constexpr float BENCH_NEAR_METRES = 10.0F
inlineconstexpr

Technique bench (Notes and plans/match_engine_technique_bench_plan.md): a catalogue of pass and shot tests, each a drill with fixed settings and tags, swept over the skill that matters (Passing, Shooting) with many seeds, on worker threads, with the sandbox's technique tuning. Results are success rates by test and skill, kept per run for CSV. A pass on the strike alone counts as on target within this many metres of the target: 1 m at 10 m and closer, 3 m at 60 m and beyond, in a straight line between.
