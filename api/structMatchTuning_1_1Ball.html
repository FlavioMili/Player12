Detailed Description

Ball physics in SI units: quadratic air drag with a drag crisis, gravity, a restitution bounce, rolling resistance on the grass and spin (sidespin and topspin/backspin) with its Magnus force.

A bounce's grip (Binturong physics): the share of the way the contact's slip (the ball's speed against its spin's) is taken toward rolling, as for a hollow sphere (I = 2/3 m r^2). Set so a ball with no spin keeps BOUNCE_TANGENTIAL_RETAINED of its speed, as before; backspin then checks it up, topspin sends it on.

◆ LIFT_PER_SPIN_RATIO

constexpr float MatchTuning::Ball::LIFT_PER_SPIN_RATIO = 1.0F
staticconstexpr

Spin (Binturong physics): the Magnus force is 0.5 rho A C_L v^2 across the ball's path, its lift coefficient C_L this many times the spin ratio (r w / v), at most MAX_LIFT (footballs: about 0.25-0.3 at spin ratios of 0.2-0.3, Goff and Carre 2010; Asai et al. 2007). Below the cap a sidespin turns the heading at a steady TURN_PER_SIDESPIN rad/s per rad/s of spin, whatever the speed (the old curve's heading rotation).
