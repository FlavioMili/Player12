Detailed Description

The match sandbox's embedded review (debugger phase 2): a timeline of the whole recorded match (both sides' phases, goals, shots, cards and substitutions) that can be scrubbed, stepped and played back, with the rebuilt moment on a pitch of its own. Live, it leaves the live match alone until the timeline is used, then pauses it; after full time it reviews the finished match.
