Detailed Description

One zone of a training session, saved (training ground plan section 23, T7f): what it was built from (its drill, settings and seed, the engine versions it ran, its players), where its players started (as carried in from the change), the commands given after its start, and a fingerprint of every tick. Replaying it rebuilds the zone alone on its own pitch on the current engine, and shows where a changed engine first plays it differently. The parallel session itself is not saved.
