◆ boardNudge()

float SupporterModel::boardNudge ( float  index)

Weekly nudge to the board's confidence: nothing within BOARD_DEAD_ZONE of 50, then linear up to +-MAX_BOARD_NUDGE at 0 and 100.

◆ evaluate()

SupporterMood SupporterModel::evaluate ( const SupporterFacts &  facts)

The mood the facts point to: results against expectation weigh most (up to +-30), then derbies (+-12), ticket prices (-12 ... +6), star sales (-9 each, at most -18) and star signings (+5 each, at most +10).
