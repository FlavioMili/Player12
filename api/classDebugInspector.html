Detailed Description

The match sandbox's inspector (debugger phase 3): what the planner, a player and the referee had decided at the moment under review, and whole-match summaries, plus the overlays drawn on the review pitch.

Every view reads the inspected engine (the rewound moment, or the live match) and the recorder's log up to that engine's tick, so the tabs rewind with the timeline. One selected player links the pitch, the Player tab and the log.
