◆ OutOfPlayId

enum class OutOfPlayId : std::uint8_t
strong

The match's "out of play": what happens when the ball leaves play and how play restarts - a goal (and who scored it), the woodwork, a corner, goal kick or throw-in, the ball settling in the net, and every restart set up and released (throw-ins, goal kicks, corners, free kicks, penalties, the taker placed). Who takes a restart, where everyone stands for it and what the taker does with the ball are the brains' (match_brain.h); how he strikes it, a direct free kick against the wall included, is his body's (match_body.h); the kick-off is shared.

AARDVARK is the inherited version, kept frozen for comparison; BINTURONG and later ones are the ones being developed. One per match, chosen before kickoff (MatchEngine::setOutOfPlay).
