Detailed Description

The match sandbox's true view of a match (Notes and plans/ match_engine_true_view_plan.md): the match screen's pitch in 3D with no figures, every player drawn as the engine models him. Every player shows his body cylinder and feet reach; two picked players (A and B) show everything, so their reaches can be seen meeting on a replay. Its settings sit in the match's side panel.
