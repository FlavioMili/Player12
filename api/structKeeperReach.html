Detailed Description

Where a ball's stretch first comes within a keeper's arms in the air (goalkeeper plan step 7): how far along (0-1), how far from his centre over the ground (m), whether both hands reach it, his stretch (0-1), the middle of his shoulders (metres, z up) and the highest his hands reach there (m).
