namespace  MatchShoutsBar
 Touchline shouts of the live match: one compact button per shout (Shift+1 .. Shift+0, Shift+- on the keyboard). The shout in force is highlighted and filled by the share of its effect that is left; shouts in quick succession have less effect, which the fill shows right away.
