Detailed Description

Touchline instructions: a shout nudges the team sliders (and shot appetite) and fades linearly; a team talk scales execution, decisions and work rate by at most a few per cent for one half. The people round the touchline (touchline plan, stage 2): each side's subs and staff seated in its dugout, and its manager in his technical area. Their bodies collide like players' (Binturong bodies only).
