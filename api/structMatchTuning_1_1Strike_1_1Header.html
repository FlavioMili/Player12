Detailed Description

Headers (step 3c): shots, clearances and knock-downs. Skill: Shooting 70%, Physicality 30%. A header is off the ground and off no foot by nature; the ball comes in fast. The old header shot had the shot's spread, and keeps it: the shot's power cost and difficulty-driven jitter (2026-10-24) are the foot's.
