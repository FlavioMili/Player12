Detailed Description

The endurance stats' physical values, lane by lane alongside the FitnessLanes of the same step (match_derived_stats.h): critical speed (m/s), burst capacity (m), the walking refill time constant (s) and the fuel capacity (s), with his walk-run transition speed (m/s) and his speed over the previous load period (m/s). Read by the Binturong body.
