float TrainingSystem::tacticalFamiliarity ( const GameData &  gamedata,
TeamID  team_id 
)

Tactical familiarity of a club with its current strategy and formation, in [0, 1] (1 = fully drilled). It grows with tactical and match-preparation sessions, matches and time, and drops when sliders or the outfield shape change. Intended match-engine use: scale positional discipline / decision noise, e.g. by 0.9 + 0.1 * familiarity.

double TrainingSystem::trainPlayer ( GameData &  gamedata,
Player &  player,

Trains one fit player of a club on day ordinal: adds load, drains condition, speeds recovery, builds sharpness. Players who played outside the club's rhythm recover first; on MD+1 the players who did not play do a compensation session.

Returns
Training-injury exposure (0 when not training), including the workload and medical staff multipliers.
