Detailed Description

Deals, loans, pre-contracts, payments and history of the market.

Owned by Game next to the world simulation. It executes structured moves (fees with instalments, add-ons and sell-on clauses, agent fees, signing bonuses, loans and releases), runs the scheduled parts of existing deals every day and drives the AI moves that do not go through the transfer list (free agents, loans, releases, pre-contracts and approaches for the managed club's players). All randomness comes from world RNG streams.
