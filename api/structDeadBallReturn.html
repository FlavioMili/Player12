Detailed Description

Binturong out of play: the ball after it has gone out (a throw-in, goal kick or corner), until the restart taker has it on the spot.

Enumerator
LOOSE 

Moving (or resting) under physics: off the boards, the walls, the nets, a throw back.

CAUGHT 

Caught by a fan in the rows the views show; thrown back soon.

OUT_OF_SIGHT 

Gone beyond the rows shown; a replacement comes back after a pause.

RETURNING_TO_SPOT 

A crowd or steward return landing at the restart spot.

CARRIED 

In the taker's hands, on its way to the spot.

LIFTED 

A throw-in: on his spot, he lifts it over his head.
