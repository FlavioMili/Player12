Detailed Description

Represents the core game state and logic.

The Game class orchestrates the simulation mechanics, handling the progression of time, saving and loading the game state, and triggering match simulations.

◆ fixMatchdaySquad()

std::size_t Game::fixMatchdaySquad ( TeamID  team_id,
MatchType  type,
std::optional< GameDateValue >  date = std::nullopt 
)

The assistant's matchday selection: regulars fit again take back their places from the players who stood in for them, then ineligible players are replaced by the best-fitting eligible squad and academy players, then by trialists, and the replacements are noted as stand-ins (Lineup::getStandIns()). When nobody fit is left for a starting place, injured players play through it (the least injured first), so a side always takes the field; players who may not play at all (suspended, on national duty) and cannot be replaced leave the XI and the side plays with the players it has. Availability is judged on date (default: today). Returns how many selections changed.

Records a managed match with its structured report (e.g. built from the live MatchEngine). A drawn cup tie is settled by extra time and penalties unless the report already carries them. consequences (MatchdaySquad::consequences of the engine) apply the measured condition and injuries exactly as for simulated matches.
