Detailed Description

Draws through a projection. Its space: x and y across the ground, z up, a direction at compass angle a (radians) being (sin a, cos a); "his right" is clockwise. The scene uses metres round the origin; a match uses the engine's own metric frame, so its handedness is the engine's.
