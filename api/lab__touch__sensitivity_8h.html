fm_lab touch-sensitivity: which variables move the first touch most (see Notes and plans/match_engine_first_touch_tuning_plan.md, section 6).

Every situation of the First touch drill, at several First Touch ratings, first as tuned (the baseline), then with one variable at a time set low and high: each tuning knob, and the receiver's own Pace, Condition, Foot and First Touch. Same seeds throughout, so the comparison is like for like. The variables are ranked by their largest effect on balls lost and on heavy touches, side by side, with the effects on the angle and weight errors beside them.

Options: –repeats N (runs per cell, default 100), –ratings A,B,... (First Touch, default 30,50,70,90), –situations all|NAME;NAME, –only NAME;NAME (variables), –step S (a knob's low and high: its value times 1 - S and 1 + S, default 0.5, kept within its range), –range "Name=low:high" (any variable's two values as given, beyond its range too; repeatable), –seed S, –out-dir DIR (report.md, cells.csv, ranking.csv; default a fresh fm-lab-touch-* folder in the temp folder), –brains and –bodies (HOME,AWAY or one for both; default binturong, whose body has the new first touch). Returns 0, or 3 for invalid options.

Whether its largest lost / heavy effects clear the noise (two standard errors of a difference at the baseline's rate).
