Detailed Description

Match audio: turns the live match into sound. Each frame the scene passes a MatchAudioFrame and the engine's event log; new events become crowd reactions, whistles and ball sounds (from the home crowd's point of view), ball launches become kicks panned by the ball's position, and the crowd bed follows the danger on the pitch. Above 4x playback the minor sounds are thinned out; a pause fades the crowd down. Purely presentational: it only reads copies and never feeds anything back into the simulation.
