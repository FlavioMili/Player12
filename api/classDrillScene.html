Detailed Description

Watch mode of a drill: its settings and results beside the pitch, played back step by step (each step runs the drill's script), restarted at will; the review, debugger and inspector windows work on the run as on a match. Space plays or pauses, R restarts. When you run the drill, the keyboard or a gamepad steers the runner through the Play controls (Esc pauses).
