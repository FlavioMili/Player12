Detailed Description

A Binturong body's taker lining up a dead ball (restart lining-up): the play he chose when his time was up (or the pass his controller asked for), the line he means to strike it along (metric unit; none for a controller's pass), and how long he has been at it. He walks to a spot just behind the ball on that line, turning to it, and kicks it once it is inside his comfort gap (so out of his dead zone), turned or out of time.
