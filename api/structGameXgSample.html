Detailed Description

Game xG (game xG plan, Notes and plans/match_engine_game_xg_plan.md): the engine's own conversion, measured by running shots in the sandbox's Shot drill and saved per layer and goal kind.

Layer 1, the empty goal: the chance a shot from a spot goes in with no keeper and nobody in the way, by the spot (metres out from the goal line, metres off the middle) and the shooter's Shooting (the 1-100 rating). A table holds a smoothed grid for lookups and the raw measured samples (so the smoothing can be redone). One measured point: shots taken from a spot at a Shooting (and, for the keeper layer, against a Goalkeeping; 0 without a keeper), and goals.
