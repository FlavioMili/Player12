Detailed Description

A strike decided at the step's start on a loose ball at his feet, played where he next meets it in the ball's sweep (unified kick plan step 3). Everything it does (offside, stats, the record) happens then; unplayed by the step's end, it is dropped and he decides again. One decided for his next meet with the ball (dribble meet plan 2a) waits for that meet, over steps, while it holds (atMeet: how long so far, and the meet it was decided for). Where a shot is aimed, in metres across and up the goal mouth.
