Detailed Description

A goal's size (metres): the same at both ends of a pitch. A match's is the Laws' (Law 1: 7.32 x 2.44 m), with its posts and net.

A goal of this kind (width x height agreed with the user, plan section 19: full size, Law 1; futsal; FA youth formats; 5-a-side common; small framed and pop-up our own estimates). Posts and net scale with the height from the full-size goal's; a pop-up goal has thin poles and a shallow net that is its frame (not researched).
