Detailed Description

Step O6, first to commit (brain redesign plan section 27): a player who goes for a loose ball looks up after his Concentration delay; with a team-mate also going, he works out which of them should take it (the clearly quicker by the precise times, else the one whose shape spot is nearest the collection point; right with a chance set by his Decisions), and calls it after his Leadership delay. The first call settles it for both. Shadow state: it changes nothing.
