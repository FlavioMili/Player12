◆ touchSkill

float MatchDuelDetail::touchSkill = 0.0F

FIRST_TOUCH: his touch skill; the speed he meant to give the ball and the speed it got (m/s); the two jobs (the turn in radians, outward or not, and the pace change in m/s); each job's chance of going wrong, whether it did, and its error (angle in radians along the turn, weight in m/s). The angle difficulty is chance, the weight's foul.
