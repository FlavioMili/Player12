Detailed Description

The core of the Binturong brain's shot score (section 24, the xG map): xG above its threshold, the have-a-go inclination (space ahead, faded in with range), the final-third bonus, Shooting, pressure, a shout, and the wide-angle discount, as the brain sums them. The brain then adds its role, cutting-inside and other terms.
