◆ INCOMING_ACROSS_SHARE

constexpr float MatchTuning::Passing::INCOMING_ACROSS_SHARE = 0.7F
staticconstexpr

Binturong body: the power budget with an incoming ball (agreed in the passing to-do, built in shooting technique plan step 6; first guesses). Kick power is what his foot gives the ball. Of the ball's pace across the line he strikes, this share stays on it and his foot aims against it; of its pace along the line, this share adds to the strike (coming at him) or takes from it (running away). The fastest he can send it: sqrt(P^2 - (across share x across pace)^2) - along share x along pace, and never under the floor share of P. Passes and shots alike (throws: their own limit).

◆ MIN_SPIN_REVS

constexpr float MatchTuning::Passing::MIN_SPIN_REVS = 4.0F
staticconstexpr

Binturong body: the most spin he can put on a pass (rev/s, sidespin and topspin together), from Passing 1 (a Sunday-league player) to Passing 99 (the world's best); a pass asking more gets this much. Skilled players' curled kicks: about 7-10 rev/s (Asai et al. 2007; Bray and Kerwin 2003).

◆ NEAR_LANE_REACH_METRES

constexpr float MatchTuning::Passing::NEAR_LANE_REACH_METRES = 1.1F
staticconstexpr

Binturong brain: a man already standing in the lane close to the passer blocks the pass without having to react to it. There the lane is only as wide as his reach (a leg and the ball), and a ball into it is almost always stopped. Further on the ball takes longer to reach him: over the ramp (about a reaction plus a stride or two at pass speed) the lane widens to the full interception radius and the risk eases to the base and late weights above.
