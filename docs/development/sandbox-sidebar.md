# Sandbox sidebar

The standalone Sandbox follows the player-profile-2 menu reference: 24px outer
padding, a fixed 220-design-pixel sidebar, a 6px content gap, square geometry,
#0F0D0B navigation background and a 6px edge fade into the #161310 shell.

Bundled Manrope Bold supplies 14px uppercase navigation labels with 1.5px
tracking. Rows are 44px high with 2px gaps, 20px line icons and cream text.
Selected rows use #2E2720, white text/icons and a 3px white left marker;
hover uses #1F1A15 and preserves the selected background. Hit targets support
keyboard navigation. Player 12 branding occupies the reference's 196 x 80 logo
area. Matches, Drills, Training and Tools retain the sandbox destinations;
Set-up is pinned beneath a footer divider as its settings destination. Save and
Load are omitted because this is the standalone sandbox.

The content pages retain their existing internal padding and theme.
Manrope's SIL OFL is bundled in assets/licenses/Manrope.txt.
