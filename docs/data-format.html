World databases (.fmdb)

A world database is one UTF-8 JSON file holding any part of a football world: countries, cities, travel times, competitions, clubs, players and name pools. The game's own world is one too: default.fmdb.

Most people never need this page. Worlds are made, combined and shared from the game: Main menu → World editor, and every new career asks which world to play in (see the World editor guide). This page is the reference for reading, reviewing or hand-editing the files, for example in a pull request or a GitHub repository of shared worlds.

Rules that always hold

  • Every section is optional except manifest. A file with only players ("legends") or only a league is valid; it is combined with other worlds in the editor, and missing squads, staff and names are generated.
  • Unknown keys are kept: the editor, combining and Export world preserve data the game does not use yet, so mods can carry their own fields.
  • The game writes files deterministically: fixed top-level order, entities sorted by id, one entity per line. Saving an unchanged world twice gives the same bytes, and changes diff line by line.
  • Ids only link entities inside one file. When worlds are combined, the game renumbers the later one; you never need to coordinate ids with other makers.
  • Files up to 64 MB are accepted. A 15,000-player world is about 5 MB.
  • Nothing is applied until a file is valid. The game lists every problem with the section, entity name and field.

Layout

{
  "manifest": {"name": "Legends", "author": "Me", "version": "1.0", "schema": 2,
               "min_game_version": "1.1.0", "season": 2026,
               "description": "Famous players of the past"},
  "languages": [{"id":1,"name":"Freelandic","first_names":["Sam","Alex"],"last_names":["Example"]}],
  "confederations": [{"id":1,"name":"Freeland Union","cups":[{"name":"Union Cup"}]}],
  "regions":   [{"id":1,"name":"The North","confederation_id":1}],
  "countries": [{"id":1,"name":"Freeland","demonym":"Freelander","language":1,"region_id":1,"reputation":60,"economy":60}],
  "cities":    [{"id":1,"name":"Northport","country_id":1,"population":50000}],
  "travel":    [{"from":1,"to":2,"minutes":90}],
  "leagues":   [{"id":1,"name":"Freeland League","country_id":1,"tiebreak":"goal_difference"}],
  "clubs":     [{"id":101,"league_id":1,"city_id":1,"name":"Northport","short_name":"NOR"}],
  "players":   [{"id":1,"team_id":101,"first_name":"Sam","last_name":"Example","age":24,"role":"ST","stats":{"Pace":70}}],
  "first_names": {"names": ["Sam"]},
  "last_names":  {"names": ["Example"]}
}

manifest.schema is 2. Files with schema: 1 (the first single-file custom databases) load unchanged: each top division becomes a country. A file with a newer schema is refused with the game version it needs (min_game_version). season is the year the ages refer to; it lets the same player be recognised in worlds made in different years.

Fields

Required fields are in bold. Ids are positive integers, unique per section.

languages (id 1-65535)

A language gives the peoples that speak it their names. Fields: name, first_names and last_names (lists; either may be left out, the general pools are used then). A country speaks a language by id, or one of the built-in languages by name ("Italian", the demonym of the built-in people whose names it uses).

confederations (id 1-65535) and regions (id 1-65535)

A region is where pre-season tours stay and what the editor calls a country's place in the world. A confederation groups regions: it plays the continental cups, hosts national-team finals and decides how far national teams travel. Worlds that declare none use the built-in ones (Europe, Eastern Europe, North America and South America in the Europe and Americas confederations); a region without confederation_id joins the first confederation, and a country without a region belongs to the first region.

FieldMeaning
region name, confederation_idIts name and confederation. base (Europe, EasternEurope, NorthAmerica, SouthAmerica) tells which built-in mix of foreign players it resembles (default: by name, else Europe).
confederation name, name_keyIts name (and a game translation key, optional).
national_finalsfalse when its nations play no continental finals (default true).
nationalitiesPeoples (demonyms) that belong to it without a country in the world. A people with a country follows the region of the country. Peoples named nowhere join the first confederation.
routesTrips of its national teams: {"to":"<confederation name>","km":16000,"hours":6}; any other pair is 24,000 km, 12 hours.
cupsIts continental cups (see below). Absent: one cup is made for it; []: none.

A cup is {"name", "name_key"?, "tier", "clubs", "matches", "places", "weekday_offsets", "final_offset", "prizes", "participation_bonus", "ranking_bonus", "knockout_bonus"}; everything but name has a default (the champions' cup for tier 1, the shield below). places are the league-phase places by association rank (the last value repeats), prizes has participation, win, draw, ranking_step, playoff, round_of_16, quarter_final, semi_final, final_fee and winner_bonus. A cup with fewer clubs available than clubs shrinks to 24 or 12. Cups take the ids from 250 up in confederation order (domestic leagues use 1-249, so a world can have as many continental competitions as it needs).

countries (id 1-65535)

FieldMeaning
nameDisplay name.
demonymThe people ("Westerosi", "Italian"): the nationality of its players. A built-in demonym (the 159 of src/global/languages.h) keeps its number in saves; any other becomes a nationality of the world (numbers after the built-in ones, up to 65535 in all). The older nationality field is read as the demonym when demonym is absent.
languageId of a language of the file, or the name of a built-in language: where the names of its players come from (general pools when absent).
region_idId of its region. The older region text (Europe, EasternEurope, NorthAmerica, SouthAmerica) still picks the region of that name.
reputationFootball level 1-100 (default 50).
economyEconomic level 1-100 (default: reputation). Drives revenue and crowds of leagues without an authored profile.

Generated players of its clubs mostly have its people as nationality. Every people takes part in national teams like a built-in one.

cities (id 1-65535) and travel

Cities have name, country_id and population. Travel rows link two cities, from and to, with minutes (1-10000); a pair appears once in either direction. Populations and travel times are stored and editable but do not affect matches or finances yet.

leagues (id 1-249)

FieldMeaning
nameDisplay name. A top division's domestic cup is named after it ("League" becomes "Cup").
country_idCountry. Without one the league gets a placeholder profile (warning).
parent_leagueDivision above; links tiers for promotion and relegation (three up, three down). A league without a parent is a top division.
tiebreakgoal_difference (default) or head_to_head.
profileEconomy and match profile (below).
round_daysOptional match-day pattern (see the built-in leagues).

profile fields are all optional; any value left out is derived from the country's reputation/economy and the division (each tier about 25 reputation points lower): reputation (1-100), average_revenue_eur, tv_share, continental_share, gate_share, commercial_share (shares 0-1), wage_ratio (wages / revenue), average_attendance, stadium_fill (0-1), domestic_nationality, domestic_share (0-1), region, coach_changes (per club and season), shape {level_sd, elite_clubs, elite_gap, level_offset} (how unequal the clubs are) and match_style {goals, yellow_cards, referee_spread, home_edge} (real match averages the engine is steered towards).

The built-in profiles are researched values: revenue, mix and wage ratios of the top five follow UEFA ECFIL 2025 (FY2024), with France after its 2024-25 TV collapse; Brazil, Russia and the USA use national reports. Second tiers use the Championship (~EUR 46m, wages above 90% of revenue), 2. Bundesliga (~EUR 50m, wages ~40%) and Serie B (EUR 24m at 82%); the rest are estimates. Crowds and stadium fill follow 2024-25 league averages; domestic shares follow CIES expatriate shares (second tiers about half the expatriates). Shapes are fitted to 20-club points curves, coach changes follow LMA/CIES counts and match styles are football-data 2022-26 averages.

FieldMeaning
nameDisplay name, unique within its league.
league_idLeague the club starts in.
city_idHome city.
short_nameThree-letter code, unique in the world.
nickname, foundedClub nickname, founding year.
colours.primary, colours.secondaryKit colours "#RRGGBB".
colours.home, .away, .thirdFull strips: shirt, shorts, socks, trim as "#RRGGBB". The badge and UI accents follow the home strip.
stadium.name, stadium.capacityStadium. The capacity used by the simulation follows the club's reputation.

players (id 1 and up)

FieldMeaning
first_name, last_nameNames. nickname is shown as First "Nick" Last.
age15-60.
rolePosition: GK, CB, LB, RB, CDM, CM, CAM, LM, RM, LW, RW or ST (the older Goalkeeper, Defender, Midfielder and Striker are accepted).
statsAttribute name → 0-100. The nine standard attributes are Pace, Shooting, Passing, Dribbling, Defending, Physicality, Stamina, Vision, Goalkeeping; missing ones take the mean of those given.
team_idClub; absent or 0 = free agent.
nationalityDemonym of a built-in people or of a country of the file; unknown values fall back to English (warning).
preferred_footLeft, Right (default) or Both.
height, skinCentimetres (100-250); skin tone 0 (lightest) - 7.
wage, contract_yearsWeekly wage, contract length.

Every club is filled up to a full squad with generated players.

Name pools

first_names and last_names each hold names (the general list), optionally by_nationality (demonym → list) and, for first names, excluded_name_hashes: full names that must never be generated, stored as 16-digit hex NamePool::nameHash values (salted FNV-1a of the lower-case, accent-folded name) so the file carries no real names. Worlds without pools use the built-in ones; combining worlds merges pools.

Combining worlds

The World editor can load several worlds into one. Nothing different is ever merged or dropped automatically; the player decides.

Two entities are the same only on a strong, unambiguous match, with exactly one candidate on each side:

  • languages, confederations, regions and countries: by name;
  • cities and leagues: by name within their country;
  • clubs: the same country, the same league (by name) and the same name;
  • players: the same name, nationality and birth year (age when the worlds have no season) at the same club (or both free agents).

Strong matches that are identical merge for convenience, but are listed on the Combine review page ("Merged automatically") with the basis of the match; for each one the player can say "these are different: keep both", which restores the incoming entity under a new id with its added children. Strong matches with different values become conflicts the player resolves (keep one, choose field by field, keep both, or use one world as the source of truth for the rest, which is always an explicit action).

Weaker matches are added as new entities and flagged as possible duplicates, never merged unless the player chooses to: a club with the same name and country in another league, a player with the same name, nationality and birth year at another club or as a free agent, keys with several candidates ("ambiguous") and similar names ("Real Madrid" and "Real Madrid CF"). Each conflict and possible duplicate shows its basis (for example name+nationality+birth_year+club, name+country, ambiguous). A world with undecided conflicts or possible duplicates cannot be saved or used to start a career until each is decided or "Keep everything as it is" is chosen.

Before a career can start

A world can be saved at any stage, but a career needs at least one league with four or more clubs, an even number of clubs in every league, no loops of parent leagues, unique three-letter codes, unique club names per league and at most 60 players per club. The editor lists what is missing.

Content rules for the repository

The built-in world keeps real city names for clubs but contains no real crests, kits, stadiums or professional players. Contributors may add their own name as a fictional player (see the player cameo guide). Share worlds based on real people and clubs from your own repositories, not from this one.
